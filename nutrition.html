Cycle Nutrition

Nutrition & Healthy Foods for Menstrual Health

Nourishing your body during your menstrual cycle replenishes essential iron lost through bleeding, supplies magnesium to relax uterine smooth muscles, and provides steady energy to stabilize moods and hormones.

⚠️ Nutritional Guidance: These recommendations focus on wholesome, accessible foods and comforting dietary habits. They are intended for educational wellness support, not to treat clinical deficiencies or replace medical advice.

Moong & Toor Dal (Lentil Soups)

Protein Sources

Light, warm, and exceptionally easy to digest. Yellow split moong and toor dal provide bioavailable plant protein and folate without stressing a sluggish menstrual digestive system.

🌱 Key Nutrients: Plant Protein, Folate, Iron
🍲 Serving Idea: Enjoy a hot bowl tempered with cumin seeds, a pinch of hing (asafoetida), and half a teaspoon of pure ghee alongside warm brown rice or soft roti.

Chickpea Chaat & Curry (Chole)

Protein Sources

Chickpeas provide sustained complex carbohydrates and essential zinc. Zinc helps regulate prostaglandins to reduce inflammation, while complex carbs stabilize blood sugar and prevent mood swings.

💪 Key Nutrients: Plant Protein, Zinc, Complex Carbs
🥗 Serving Idea: Toss boiled chickpeas with diced cucumber, tomatoes, a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, roasted jeera, and fresh coriander for a nourishing snack.

Rajma (Red Kidney Beans)

Protein Sources

A comforting, mineral-dense staple packed with non-heme iron and magnesium. Helps restore hemoglobin levels while relaxing contracted uterine muscles during heavy flow days.

🩸 Key Nutrients: Dietary Iron, Magnesium, Fiber
🍛 Serving Idea: Slow-simmered rajma curry cooked with fresh tomatoes, ginger, and mild spices, served warm with a portion of steamed rice or whole wheat chapati.

Palak & Methi (Spinach & Fenugreek)

Iron-Rich

Dark leafy greens are foundational for counteracting menstrual blood loss and preventing mid-cycle fatigue, brain fog, and low hemoglobin levels.

🥬 Key Nutrients: Non-Heme Iron, Vitamin K, Folate
🍋 Serving Idea: Sauté lightly with minced garlic and always finish with fresh lemon juice—vitamin C doubles non-heme plant iron absorption in your gut!

Beetroot & Carrot Salad

Iron-Rich

Naturally rich in plant iron, dietary nitrates, and beta-carotene. Beetroots promote microvascular circulation, boost cellular oxygenation, and support hepatic estrogen clearance.

🌿 Key Nutrients: Iron, Dietary Nitrates, Vitamin A
🥗 Serving Idea: Grate fresh beetroot and carrots together, add fresh mint, roasted pumpkin seeds, and a light dressing of lemon juice and Himalayan pink salt.

Curd & Spiced Chaas (Buttermilk)

Calcium & Magnesium

Calcium helps suppress excess prostaglandin synthesis to calm painful uterine cramping, while live probiotics alleviate hormonal bloating, indigestion, and sluggish bowels.

🥛 Key Nutrients: Bioavailable Calcium, Probiotics, B12
🧂 Serving Idea: Whisk fresh homemade curd with cool water, roasted cumin powder, black salt, and crushed mint for a deeply refreshing post-lunch digestive tonic.

Fresh Paneer (Cottage Cheese)

Calcium & Magnesium

A concentrated source of calcium, phosphorus, and slow-digesting casein protein that curbs intense cravings, stabilizes satiety, and prevents sudden blood sugar crashes.

🧀 Key Nutrients: Complete Protein, Calcium, Phosphorus
🧄 Serving Idea: Lightly pan-sear paneer cubes with turmeric and black pepper, or toss into a warm paneer bhurji with onions, tomatoes, and whole wheat rotis.

Boiled Farm Eggs

Protein Sources

A complete protein source containing all 9 essential amino acids, choline for liver hormone clearance, vitamin D, and heme iron that is rapidly absorbed by red blood cells.

🥚 Key Nutrients: Complete Protein, Choline, Vitamin D
🍳 Serving Idea: Enjoy 1 to 2 boiled eggs sprinkled with black pepper and pink salt alongside avocado toast or steamed greens for an energizing breakfast.

Fresh Pomegranate (Anar)

Fruits & Veggies

Rich in punicalagins, polyphenols, and vitamin C. Helps neutralize inflammatory oxidative stress, protects cardiovascular capillaries, and promotes healthy blood building.

🫐 Key Nutrients: Polyphenols, Vitamin C, Potassium
🥣 Serving Idea: Sprinkle juicy ruby-red pomegranate arils over morning oatmeal, homemade chaas, or enjoy a fresh bowl as a mid-morning energy boost.

Bananas & Sweet Papaya

Fruits & Veggies

Potassium naturally flushes out excess water retention and reduces menstrual puffiness, while vitamin B6 promotes serotonin synthesis and ripe papaya enzymes ease cramping.

🍌 Key Nutrients: Potassium, Vitamin B6, Papain
🍽️ Serving Idea: Slice a ripe banana with peanut butter on multigrain toast, or enjoy a bowl of chilled sweet papaya cubes between main meals.

Roasted Pumpkin & Sesame Seeds (Til)

Calcium & Magnesium

Pumpkin seeds are among nature's highest dietary sources of magnesium to relax contracted uterine muscles, while sesame seeds deliver concentrated plant calcium and zinc.

🌻 Key Nutrients: Magnesium, Zinc, Lignans, Calcium
✨ Serving Idea: Dry-roast pumpkin seeds with a pinch of sea salt for a crunchy desk snack, or enjoy traditional homemade til-gur ladoos in moderation.

Soaked Almonds & Walnuts

Calcium & Magnesium

Plant-based omega-3 fatty acids and vitamin E down-regulate inflammatory prostaglandins, effectively reducing the throbbing intensity of menstrual cramps.

🌰 Key Nutrients: Vitamin E, ALA Omega-3s, Magnesium
💧 Serving Idea: Soak 5 to 6 almonds overnight, peel in the morning, and eat alongside 2 walnut halves before having morning tea or breakfast.

Tender Coconut Water (Nariyal Pani)

Hydration & Teas

Nature's purest electrolyte beverage. Packed with natural potassium, magnesium, and bioavailable fluid to quickly combat period dehydration, fatigue, and headaches.

🥥 Key Nutrients: Natural Potassium, Magnesium, Electrolytes
🌴 Serving Idea: Drink fresh coconut water at room temperature in the late morning, especially during heavy flow days when minerals deplete.

Warm Ginger & Tulsi Tea

Hydration & Teas

Active gingerols in fresh ginger inhibit the cyclooxygenase enzyme pathway, clinically demonstrating cramp relief comparable to mild over-the-counter pain relievers.

🫖 Key Nutrients: Gingerols, Eugenol, Anti-Inflammatory Oils
🍯 Serving Idea: Crush fresh ginger root and tulsi leaves into boiling water, simmer for 5 minutes, strain into a mug, and add a teaspoon of honey.

Chamomile & Peppermint Tea

Hydration & Teas

Chamomile delivers apigenin to soothe nervous tension and invite deep sleep, while peppermint's natural menthol relaxes smooth intestinal muscles to ease menstrual gas and bloating.

🌼 Key Nutrients: Apigenin, Menthol, Antispasmodic Bioflavonoids
🌙 Serving Idea: Steep a warm cup 30 to 45 minutes before bedtime to unwind your pelvic nerves and prepare for deep restorative rest.

Vegetable Moong Dal Khichdi

Balanced Meals

The quintessential Ayurvedic comfort food: provides a complete amino acid profile, is exceptionally gentle on delicate digestive tracts, and provides steady restorative warmth.

🍲 Key Nutrients: Complete Protein, Complex Carbs, Butyrate
🥄 Serving Idea: Prepare soft khichdi with split yellow moong, carrots, peas, and mild turmeric; finish with 1 teaspoon of warm A2 desi ghee and fresh curd.
