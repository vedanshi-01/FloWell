Menstrual Health Do’s & Don’ts

Caring for your body during your cycle should be gentle, empowering, and grounded in medical facts. Here is a clear guide on healthy supportive habits and common practices to avoid.

← Back to Dashboard

✅ Things to Do

💧

Hydrate Consistently (2–3 Liters Daily)

Drinking adequate water prevents fluid retention and relieves abdominal bloating and cyclical dehydration headaches.

💡 Practical Tip: Sip room-temperature water or warm herbal infusions steadily throughout the day.
⏱️

Change Menstrual Products Regularly

Replace sanitary pads or tampons every 4 to 8 hours, and empty menstrual cups every 8 to 12 hours, regardless of how light the flow seems.

💡 Practical Tip: Setting subtle calendar alerts on busy workdays ensures timely freshening.
🚿

Wash Gently with Plain Warm Water

Cleanse only the external vulva from front to back using plain warm water. The internal vagina maintains its own healthy acidic flora.

💡 Practical Tip: Avoid scented soaps, deodorants, or bubble baths near your intimate areas.
🔥

Apply Soothing Heat for Cramps

Placing a heating pad or hot water bottle (approx. 40°C) over the lower abdomen stimulates vasodilation and relaxes uterine smooth muscle.

💡 Practical Tip: 15–20 minutes of gentle heat therapy can match the relief of mild over-the-counter analgesics.
😴

Prioritize 7 to 9 Hours of Sleep

Quality restorative sleep allows your nervous system to regulate pain thresholds, lower inflammatory prostaglandins, and stabilize moods.

💡 Practical Tip: Sleeping on your side in a gentle fetal tuck with a cushion between knees eases pelvic tension.
🚶‍♀️

Engage in Gentle, Low-Impact Movement

Light walking, restorative stretching, and gentle yoga release beta-endorphins—your body’s natural painkillers—without exhausting you.

💡 Practical Tip: A 15-minute relaxed stroll in fresh air clears brain fog and relieves pelvic congestion.
🍲

Eat Regular, Balanced Meals

Fueling your body with complex carbs, protein, and dietary fiber prevents hypoglycemic dips that exacerbate PMS irritability and fatigue.

💡 Practical Tip: Incorporate warm dal, oats, curd, or steamed vegetables rather than skipping breakfast.
🥬

Replenish Iron and Magnesium

Menstrual blood loss depletes iron stores. Consuming lentils, leafy greens, seeds, and dark chocolate restores stamina and eases spasms.

💡 Practical Tip: Squeeze fresh lemon juice over plant-based iron foods to quadruple iron absorption via Vitamin C.
📱

Track Symptoms & Cycle Milestones

Recording start dates, duration, cramp intensity, and emotional changes helps you recognize patterns and predict future cycles accurately.

💡 Practical Tip: FloWell’s tracker and history logger makes spotting cycle variations simple and private.
🩲

Wear Breathable 100% Cotton Underwear

Breathable natural cotton fabric permits optimal air circulation, preventing heat and sweat buildup that can invite yeast overgrowth.

💡 Practical Tip: Choose loose, seamless cotton cuts over tight synthetic nylon during active bleeding days.
🧘‍♀️

Practice Mindful Stress Reduction

Elevated psychological stress triggers cortisol release, which can disrupt luteinizing hormone signals and amplify cramp severity.

💡 Practical Tip: Try 4-7-8 breathing: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, and exhale slowly for 8 seconds.
👜

Pack an Emergency Period Care Kit

Carrying a small pouch with extra pads, liners, pain relief, and wet wipes provides peace of mind whenever your period starts unexpectedly.

💡 Practical Tip: Keep one prepared pouch in your work backpack and another in your travel tote.
🛀

Take Warm Showers or Baths

Warm water cascading over your back and abdomen stimulates peripheral blood flow, relaxes stiff muscles, and enhances overall comfort.

💡 Practical Tip: A warm evening shower before bed encourages deeper, more restful sleep.
🩺

Consult a Doctor for Severe Symptoms

Schedule a gynecological consultation if you experience bleeding that soaks through a pad every hour or pain that disrupts daily living.

💡 Practical Tip: Document dates and symptom severity to share an accurate timeline with your physician.

❌ Things to Avoid

🚫

Don’t Use Vaginal Douches or Scented Sprays

Internal douching strips away the protective acidic microbiome, drastically increasing susceptibility to bacterial vaginosis and yeast infections.

⚠️ Why It Matters: The vagina is naturally self-cleansing; only wash external skin with plain water.
⚠️

Don’t Leave Tampons in Beyond 8 Hours

Leaving tampons inserted for excessive durations fosters bacterial colonization, significantly raising the risk of Toxic Shock Syndrome (TSS).

⚠️ Why It Matters: Switch to pads before going to sleep overnight if you sleep longer than 7–8 hours.
🙅‍♀️

Don’t Normalize Debilitating, Bed-Ridden Pain

Pain that makes you vomit, faint, or miss school/work is not "just a normal period." It may indicate endometriosis, adenomyosis, or fibroids.

⚠️ Why It Matters: You deserve effective medical management; never let anyone dismiss severe agony.
🍽️

Don’t Skip Meals Due to Nausea or Cramps

Prolonged fasting causes rapid blood sugar drops, exacerbating nausea, fatigue, headache throbbing, and emotional irritability.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Even small, bland snacks like plain toast, banana slices, or khichdi help stabilize energy.
☕

Don’t Overconsume Excessive Caffeine

High doses of caffeine act as a vasoconstrictor, narrowing blood vessels in the pelvis and potentially intensifying uterine contractions.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Swap that 3rd or 4th cup of espresso for soothing chamomile, ginger, or peppermint tea.
🧂

Don’t Binge on Ultra-Salty Processed Snacks

Excess dietary sodium prompts water retention in extracellular tissues, exacerbating heavy abdominal bloating and painful breast tenderness.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Opt for roasted unsalted nuts, fresh fruits, or light home-roasted makhana instead.
🏋️‍♀️

Don’t Force Exhausting High-Intensity Workouts

Forcing heavy deadlifts, maximum sprints, or extreme HIIT during heavy bleeding days stresses adrenal glands and heightens fatigue.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Exercise is optional and should adapt to comfort; honoring your need to rest is healthy.
🚿

Don’t Believe Bathing or Showering is Unsafe

Old myths claiming you should not bathe or wash hair during your period are completely false. Warm water is safe, hygienic, and comforting.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Cleanliness prevents odor and bacterial build-up while soothing aching muscles.
🚽

Don’t Flush Menstrual Products in the Toilet

Pads, tampons, and wipes do not disintegrate like toilet paper. Flushing them causes severe plumbing blockages and environmental pollution.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Wrap used products securely in newspaper or biodegradable bags and discard in trash bins.
💊

Don’t Misuse Unprescribed Pain Medications

Never exceed recommended dosages or take NSAIDs on an empty stomach, as this can irritate gastric lining and trigger heartburn or ulcers.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Always take anti-inflammatory medications with food or milk, as directed by a healthcare professional.
🩲

Don’t Wear Damp Products to "Save Them"

Wearing a single pad all day because "flow is light" traps sweat, moisture, and bacteria against the skin, inviting chafing and rashes.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Change pads at least 3–4 times daily, regardless of the discharge volume.
🤫

Don’t Suffer in Silence or Feel Ashamed

Menstruation is a natural, vital sign of reproductive health. There is zero reason to feel embarrassed, unclean, or isolated.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Communicating your needs with partners, friends, and doctors promotes well-being and empathy.
🧪

Don’t Rely on Unverified "Period Hack" Tonics

Beware of viral social media recipes claiming to instantly stop bleeding, induce periods, or cure cysts with unregulated concoctions.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Consuming unverified herbs in large doses can cause gastric poisoning or dangerous hormonal chaos.
📅

Don’t Ignore Prolonged Irregular Cycles

If your period consistently arrives faster than 21 days, vanishes for 90+ days (not pregnant), or bleeds for 10+ days, consult a doctor.

⚠️ Why It Matters: Early diagnosis of conditions like PCOS or thyroid imbalance ensures timely, effective care.
